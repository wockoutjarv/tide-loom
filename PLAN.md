# Tide Loom

A pocket-sized puzzle about weaving a tide chart. Each loom has three overlapping pedals. A pedal raises three floats by one mark on a three-mark cycle. Match the target in the fewest pulls. The tide and target use numerals as well as shape and colour.

Prototype acceptance: every level has a unique solution in pedal counts modulo three, starts unsolved and accepts a win only when every float matches. Play with touch or a keyboard. Undo should restore the exact prior board. See QA.md for independent review findings.
