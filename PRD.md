# Tide Loom requirements

Players solve 26 short puzzles by matching six floats to visible targets. Each of three pedals advances three named positions around a three-step cycle. All targets must be reachable and distinct. Advance only on an exact match. Undo must restore the previous board, including after a win. Completing the last puzzle offers a replay. Provide labelled floats, keyboard-operable controls, narrow-screen layout and offline play without accounts or tracking.
